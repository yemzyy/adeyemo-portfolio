FAQ

I offer graphic design and video editing services. My graphic design work includes social media graphics, flyers, promotional designs, carousel posts, branding visuals, and other creative content. For video editing, I specialize in short-form content, social media videos, promotional videos, and engaging edits designed to capture and hold attention.

The turnaround time depends on the type and scope of the project. Simple designs and short videos can be completed faster, while larger projects or multiple deliverables may require more time. I’ll discuss the expected delivery time with you before the project begins.

I work with professional creative tools including Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe Photoshop, Canva, CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, and other tools depending on the requirements of the project.

Revisions depend on the project and the service selected. We’ll agree on the revision terms before work begins so you know what is included. Additional revisions or major changes outside the original project scope may require an additional charge.

You can send your files through a file-sharing service or provide a link to where your files are stored. When you submit an enquiry, I’ll let you know the best way to send the materials for your specific project.

For graphic design, final files can be delivered in formats such as JPG, PNG, PDF, or other formats suited to your needs. For video editing, I typically deliver high-quality MP4 files, with the resolution and aspect ratio agreed on for the project.

Start by filling out the contact form and providing details about your project, including the service you need, your requirements, budget, and deadline. I’ll review your enquiry and get back to you so we can discuss the project and next steps.

I currently accept payments in Nigerian naira (₦) and U.S. dollars ($). Payment details and the preferred payment method will be discussed and agreed upon before the project begins. Depending on your location, we can use a suitable payment platform or transfer method that works for both of us. For international payments, I can also receive payments through supported online payment platforms. If you have a preferred payment method, feel free to mention it when submitting your enquiry, and we can agree on the most convenient option.
