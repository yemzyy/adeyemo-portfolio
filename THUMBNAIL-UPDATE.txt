PERMANENT VIDEO THUMBNAILS + VISIBLE-PLAYER QUEUE

Included: real Canva covers for all 52 videos and 8 vlogs, saved as local JPEGs
in assets/video-thumbnails (1,473,450 bytes combined). They are served with the
GitHub website, not fetched from temporary Canva image URLs at visitor runtime.

Covers remain in the card when an old player is evicted, while a replacement
player waits in the queue, and during initial iframe loading. No extra Play
overlay is added: the ready Canva player keeps its own controls and thumbnail.
The cover itself is not an additional clickable playback button.

Only two initial Canva iframe loads run simultaneously. Eligible cards must be
at least 25% visible; the nearest to the screen centre are prioritized. Unfinished
loads that leave the screen are cancelled so they cannot hold up visible cards.
A slot is released on the iframe load event or its existing 30-second timeout.
That event does not prove Canva has finished buffering the actual video.
Loaded players retain the existing bounded cache and recently focused protection.

Canva remains primary. The existing best-effort Cloudinary startup fallback,
local silent hero playlist, link corrections and image backups remain in place.
Vlog 08 still has no supplied Cloudinary backup URL.

Verification: all 60 poster files exist, match the JSON and embedded defaults,
and total about 1.5 MB. Browser checks confirmed local covers while only two
players initially loaded; visible players subsequently completed the queue;
scrolling away/back preserved loaded thumbnails; one Canva Play click started
video 01 (paused=false, readyState=4, currentTime advancing).

Limits: this reduces empty/loading previews and competing iframe starts. It
cannot guarantee instant Canva playback or repair freezes inside Canva's player.
